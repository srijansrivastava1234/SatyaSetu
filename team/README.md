## Leadership & Stage Dynamics Strategy
A winning hackathon pitch separates strategic narrative from technical operations to maximize judge impact:
- **Anshika Pandey (Team Leader)** controls the stage:
  - Delivers the word-for-word 5-minute pitch narrative with complete stage presence and judge eye contact.
  - Controls the stage physical pacing and audience engagement.
  - Carries structured reference cards with key metrics, architecture schematics, and problem framing.
  - Runs the stopwatch with silent tactile cues to strictly enforce the 5-minute hard limit.
  - Intercepts all judge Q&A questions as the single point of entry and strategically routes deep technical specifics.
- **Srijan, Amit, Akhilesh, and Suryansh** manage live technical execution, ensuring the servers, models, UI, and live demo run without failure.
