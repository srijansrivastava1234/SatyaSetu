## Why Having 1 Member with No Laptop is an Asset, Not a Liability
Dividing attention between code debugging and presentation delivery guarantees a weak pitch.  
- **Members 1 to 4** have their heads down on machines, ensuring the servers, models, UI, and extension run without failure.
- **Member 5** is unencumbered by a laptop:
  - Memorizes the word-for-word 5-minute pitch narrative.
  - Controls the stage physical presence and eye contact with judges.
  - Carries physical index cards with key metrics and architecture schematics.
  - Runs the stopwatch with silent vibration signals to enforce the 5-minute hard limit.
  - Intercepts all judge Q&A questions as the single point of entry.
