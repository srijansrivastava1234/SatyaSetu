# Member 3 Presentation Defense: Accessibility, UX & Inclusivity
*Focus: Alignment with Hackathon Judging Criteria ("Practicality, UX and Inclusivity")*

---

### Key Defense Talking Points:

1. **Cognitive Load Reduction**:
   - Traditional fact-checking portals (Snopes, AltNews) present 1,500-word investigative articles that rural or semi-literate smartphone users will never read before forwarding a message.
   - SatyaSetu distills complex verification into three intuitive visual signals:
     - Clear Color-Coded Verdict: Red (`FALSE`), Green (`VERIFIED`), Amber (`UNVERIFIABLE`).
     - Gauge Meters for Deception: Visual speedometers showing panic/urgency level at a glance.
     - Dual-Language WhatsApp Rebuttal: Pre-formatted with bold tags ready to paste back into family groups in Hindi and English.

2. **High-Contrast Auditorium Display Standard**:
   - Tuned with high-contrast Tailwind tokens ensuring text and scorecards are 100% legible even on washed-out auditorium projectors.
   - Standardized typography and large tap targets calibrated for both projector zoom (125%-150%) and mobile viewport rendering.

3. **Inclusivity for the Next Billion Users**:
   - Citizens in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities communicate predominantly via Indic languages. The UI provides instant toggling between English and Hindi, ensuring that digital truth is not gated behind English-language proficiency.
